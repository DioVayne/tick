# Creating your own account

Some Ticks are open to everyone, like the Tick behind the Android app in Google Play. There you make your own account. On a Tick that someone runs for their household or team, you get an invitation link instead (see [Sharing](sharing.md#accounts)).

## Creating an account
1. Open Tick. The welcome screen shows the Tick logo, **Sign in** and **Create account**.
2. Tap **Create account** and fill in your name, email address and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. You are in Tick straight away, with an empty list.

Already have an account with that email address? Tick says so, with links to sign in and to **Forgot password?**.

## Confirming your email address
Right after you sign up, Tick sends you an email with a link. Tap it to confirm that the address is yours.

- You can use Tick before you confirm. Only **sharing** waits: you can't share tasks or lists, and invitations from others don't reach you yet.
- Until you confirm, a bar at the top of Tick says so, with **Send email again**. The link works for 7 days; a new email gives a new link.
- An account that is not confirmed within **30 days** is deleted, with everything in it. The bar shows the date.
- Choosing a new password through **Forgot password?** also confirms your address, because that email reached you too.
- If you change your email address under **⚙ Settings → Account**, you confirm the new one the same way.

## Nobody sees who else is on this Tick
On an open Tick, people can't see each other's names or addresses. You share by typing someone's full email address (see [Sharing](sharing.md#on-an-open-tick)). People you already share a task or list with appear as a button in the share dialog, so you don't have to type them again.

## Deleting your account
Under **⚙ Settings → Account → Delete account**. Everything is removed right away. See [Privacy](privacy.md).
