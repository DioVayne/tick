# iPhone and iPad

There is no Tick app in the App Store. You don't need one: put Tick on your home screen from Safari and it opens full screen with its own icon and sends notifications, like an app. It updates itself, so there is nothing to download.

## Putting Tick on your home screen
1. Open the address of your Tick in **Safari** and log in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open Tick with the new icon, not from Safari.

The home-screen Tick has its own login, separate from Safari, so you may need to log in once more.

## Notifications
On an iPhone or iPad, notifications only work in the home-screen Tick (iOS 16.4 or newer).
1. Open Tick from the home-screen icon.
2. Go to **⚙ Settings → Notifications**, turn them on and allow them when iOS asks.
3. Tap *Send test notification* to check.

If you change your mind later, notifications for Tick are also in the iPhone's own **Settings → Notifications → Tick**.

## What the iPhone cannot do
Apple gives a home-screen app less room than the [Android app](android.md) has. On an iPhone or iPad there is:
- **no place reminders:** you can set a [place](places.md) on a task, but only an Android phone with the app notifies you when you arrive or leave;
- **no widget** and no quick add from the share menu of other apps.

Everything else (tasks, lists, reminders, the calendar, sharing, [driving mode](driving-mode.md)) works the same as in the browser.

## Why not an App Store app
An App Store app needs a paid Apple developer account and has to go through Apple's review for every update. The home-screen Tick does the same for a to-do app without either.
