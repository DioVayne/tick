# Terms of service

An open Tick (one where you make your own account, like the Tick behind the Android app in Google Play) has terms of service. By creating an account you agree to them; the sign-up screen links to them.

- In English: `/terms` on your Tick's address, for example `https://app.ticktodo.nl/terms`.
- In Dutch: `/voorwaarden`, for example `https://app.ticktodo.nl/voorwaarden`.

The terms say, in plain language, who offers Tick and how to reach them, that you must be 16 or older, what is and isn't allowed, how possible paid features and the EU right of withdrawal work, what happens when you or the provider stops, the limits of liability (while Tick is free you use it at your own risk), and that Dutch law applies. How Tick handles your data is on the [Privacy](privacy.md) page.

A Tick that someone runs for their household or team has no terms of service: there, `/terms` and `/voorwaarden` don't exist.

## For the person running an open Tick
The terms show the name from `OPERATOR_NAME`, and when set, the address from `OPERATOR_ADDRESS` and the Chamber of Commerce number from `OPERATOR_KVK`, plus the `ADMIN_CONTACT` address (see `wrangler.example.jsonc`). The text is written for a Tick run from the Netherlands; the date at the top is part of the text in `public/i18n.js`.
