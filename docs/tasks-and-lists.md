# Tasks and lists

## Adding a task
Type in the field at the top of a list and press Enter. **Shift+Enter** (or the *Details* button) adds the task and opens it right away. A new task goes into the list you are looking at; in *All* it goes into the Inbox.

Open a task to give it a reminder, a place, notes, subtasks or attachments. On a wide screen the task opens in a panel on the right (drag its edge to make it wider or narrower); on a phone it fills the screen and **‹** or the back button takes you back. Title and notes save while you type.

Tick off a task with the box in front of it. A ticked-off task goes to **Completed**; tick the box again there to reopen it. Right after ticking off, *Undo* shows at the bottom for a few seconds.

## Subtasks
A task can have one level of subtasks. A subtask follows its parent: same list, and ticking off the parent ticks off its subtasks.

## Lists
- **Inbox** is always there and cannot be renamed or deleted.
- **+ New list** in the sidebar makes a list; it stays even while empty.
- Rename, merge or delete a list with right-click, a long press, **⋯**, or (on a phone) by tapping the list name at the top. Renaming to a name that already exists merges the two lists. Deleting asks whether the tasks go to the Inbox or to the trash.
- Every list has its own colour, shown in the sidebar and on the list pill of a task.
- **All** shows the open tasks of every list together.
- A list can be shared with others, so you all work in the same list: see [Sharing a list](sharing.md#sharing-a-list).

## Order, pinning, dragging and swiping
- **Drag** a task to change the order: with a mouse by its **⋮⋮** handle, on a touch screen by holding the task (the checkbox works too) for a moment and then dragging (a quick swipe up or down still scrolls, sideways still swipes). On a wide screen you can also drop it on a list in the sidebar to move it, or on **Trash** to delete it.
- **Pin** a task to keep it at the top of its list. Only top-level tasks can be pinned.
- **Swipe** (phone): to the left deletes the task, to the right puts it in another list. *Undo* shows for a few seconds.

## Search
The magnifying glass (or **/** on a keyboard) searches titles and notes, completed tasks included. Escape clears the search. A matching subtask shows its parent.

## Notes
The notes of a task are markdown: headings, **bold**, *italics*, lists, links, quotes, code and checklists. A checklist line (`- [ ] milk`) becomes a box you can tick in the read view. Tables, images and HTML are shown as plain text.

## Attachments (optional)
When attachments are set up ([SETUP.md, step 11](../SETUP.md#11-optional-attachments)), a task gets **📎 Add photo or file**. You can also paste or drop files on the task. Photos are scaled down and lose their EXIF data (such as where they were taken) before they are uploaded. Tap a photo for a large view with previous/next, Download and Delete.

- At most 20 files per task, each up to 10 MB unless your Tick sets another limit.
- Deleting an attachment cannot be undone.
- Attachments need a connection; photos you have already seen stay visible offline.
- A backup file lists attachments but does not contain them.

## The trash
Deleting a task moves it to **Trash**, together with its subtasks. Tasks stay there for 30 days; restore them, delete them for good, or empty the trash. Emptying deletes only the tasks you own.

## Offline
Tick works without a connection once you have opened it online and logged in. Changes are saved on the device and sent when you are back online; if two devices changed the same field, the last change wins. Sharing, the trash, attachments and the map need a connection.

## Bookmarks
The address in the browser follows what you see (`#/inbox`, `#/calendar`, `#/task/…`), so you can bookmark a list, the calendar or a single task.
