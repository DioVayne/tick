# Tasks and lists

## Adding a task
Type in the field at the top of a list (or press **N**) and press Enter. A new task goes into the list you are looking at; in *All* it goes into the Inbox.

When you tap the field, a row of small colored icons shows under it (reminder, repeat, place, list, share, more). Tap one to set that first and type the title after. As soon as you type (or pick something), the field opens into a card with a short line for each thing you can add straight away:
- **Remind me…** offers *Today*, *Tomorrow* and *Next week*; *Other day and time…* opens the full date and time picker.
- **Repeat** opens the same choices as in a task (every day, every week, and so on).
- **Remind me at a place** opens the map, with your favorites, search and *On arrival* or *On leaving*. See [Places](places.md).
- **In Inbox** shows where the task goes; tap it to pick another list.
- **Do it together with…** picks someone on this Tick, or a name or email address. Not shown in a shared list, where everyone is in already.
- **Description, subtasks or attachment** adds three more lines: a description (Enter starts a new line), subtasks (Enter adds one), and a photo or file. You can also paste a picture or drop a file on the card.

A line you filled in shows your choice in color, with *Clear* behind it. Press Enter in the title (or **Add**) to add the task; the field stays ready for the next one. **Shift+Enter** adds the task and opens it right away. **✕** (or **Esc**) empties the card and closes it. On a phone, *Cancel* or the back button closes a picker without changing anything.

Sharing and attachments are sent once the task is on your Tick, so they need a connection. Offline, Tick adds the task and tells you to add those from the task later.

Open a task to give it a reminder, a place, notes, subtasks or attachments. On a wide screen the task opens in a panel on the right (drag its edge to make it wider or narrower); on a phone it fills the screen and **‹** or the back button takes you back. Title and notes save while you type.

The task looks like the card you added it with. Under the title is the same line of sentences, each with its colored dot: **Remind me…**, **Repeat**, **Remind me at a place**, **In Inbox** and **Do it together with…**. A sentence in grey is not set yet; tap it to set it, with the same pickers as in the card. Something you set shows in color, with *Clear* behind it; the reminder also has **In 1 hour**, which moves it an hour later. Under a place it says when you were already notified, with **Notify again**. Who you share the task with is listed under *Together with*, with *Remove* or *Leave*. In a shared list the line **Notify me when this is ticked off** takes the place of sharing. A subtask has no *Repeat*, and its list says it follows the parent task.

Below the sentences are the **Description**, **Subtasks** and **Attachments**. A completed task shows only what it has, and you cannot change it until you reopen it.

Tick off a task with the box in front of it. The task drops straight to the bottom of the list, in reversed colours with *Undo* on it and a thin bar that runs out in five seconds; then it goes to **Completed**. The rest of the list moves up at once, so you can tick off the next task right away. In a long list the ticked-off tasks stay in view at the bottom of the screen; with more than three at once the oldest fold into one line (**+2 done**) that opens them again. A ticked-off subtask drops to the bottom of its parent, and a repeating task stays where it is (it moves on to its next time). In **Completed**, tick the box again to reopen a task.

## Subtasks
A task can have one level of subtasks. A subtask follows its parent: same list, and ticking off the parent ticks off its subtasks. In the list, a task and its subtasks share one grey block: the subtasks are smaller and lighter, and a thin line runs from the parent's box to each subtask, so you can see what belongs together. Next to the parent's title a small counter such as **2/4** shows how many of its subtasks are done.

## Lists
- **Inbox** is always there and cannot be renamed or deleted.
- **+ New list** in the sidebar makes a list; it stays even while empty.
- Rename, merge or delete a list with right-click, a long press, **⋯**, or (on a phone) by tapping the list name at the top. Renaming to a name that already exists merges the two lists. Deleting asks whether the tasks go to the Inbox or to the trash.
- Every list has its own colour, shown in the sidebar and on the list pill of a task.
- **All** shows the open tasks of every list together.
- A list can be shared with others, so you all work in the same list: see [Sharing a list](sharing.md#sharing-a-list).

## Order, pinning, dragging and swiping
- **Drag** a task to change the order: with a mouse, press anywhere on the task and drag (a click without moving still opens it); on a touch screen, hold the task (the checkbox works too) for a moment and then drag (a quick swipe up or down still scrolls, sideways still swipes), or drag the **⋮⋮** handle straight away. On a wide screen you can also drop it on a list in the sidebar to move it, or on **Trash** to delete it.
- **Pin** a task to keep it at the top of its list. Only top-level tasks can be pinned.
- **Swipe** (phone): to the left deletes the task, to the right puts it in another list. *Undo* shows for a few seconds.

## Search
The magnifying glass (or **/** on a keyboard) searches titles and notes, completed tasks included. Escape clears the search. A matching subtask shows its parent.

## Notes
The notes of a task are markdown: headings, **bold**, *italics*, lists, links, quotes, code and checklists. A checklist line (`- [ ] milk`) becomes a box you can tick in the read view. Tables, images and HTML are shown as plain text.

## Attachments (optional)
When attachments are set up ([SETUP.md, step 11](../SETUP.md#11-optional-attachments)), a task gets **📎 Add photo or file**. You can also paste or drop files on the task. Photos are scaled down and lose their EXIF data (such as where they were taken) before they are uploaded. Tap a photo for a large view with previous/next, Download and Delete.

- At most 20 files per task, each up to 10 MB unless your Tick sets another limit.
- Your Tick may also limit the total per account (for example 100 MB). Settings › Data › **Storage** shows how much you use. What you upload to a task someone shared with you counts for you.
- Deleting an attachment cannot be undone.
- Attachments need a connection; photos you have already seen stay visible offline.
- A backup file lists attachments but does not contain them.

## The trash
Deleting a task moves it to **Trash**, together with its subtasks. Tasks stay there for 30 days; restore them, delete them for good, or empty the trash. Emptying deletes only the tasks you own.

## Offline
Tick works without a connection once you have opened it online and logged in. Changes are saved on the device and sent, in order, when you are back online. While something is waiting, the list says *Saving…*. Sharing, the trash, attachments and the map need a connection.

### Editing on several devices
Tick does not merge edits. Each change you make is sent as "set this field to this value", and the change that **reaches your Tick last** wins, per field:
- Edit the title on your phone while it is offline, and the reminder on your laptop: both stick, because they are different fields.
- Edit the **same** field on both (for example the title, or the description), and the device that comes back online last overwrites the other, even if its edit was made earlier. The description is one field: the other device's text is replaced, not combined.
- The other device shows the result the next time it refreshes (on opening Tick, or when it comes back online).

Some things are protected:
- **Ticking off a repeating task** is sent for one occurrence. If another device already moved the task on, the late tick does nothing, so an occurrence is never skipped twice.
- A change to a task that was **deleted** on another device is dropped without a message.
- **"I'll take it"** in a shared list: if someone else took the task first, yours is refused with a message.

### "Could not be saved"
If your Tick keeps answering with an error to one change (5 tries, for more than 10 minutes), Tick sets that change aside so the changes after it can still go through, and tells you: *A change to "…" could not be saved*. The list then shows what your Tick actually has, so check that task and make the change again. Changes that depend on it (for example a subtask under a new task that could not be saved) are dropped with it, without a message each. Being offline never counts: changes made offline wait as long as needed.

## Bookmarks
The address in the browser follows what you see (`#/inbox`, `#/calendar`, `#/task/…`), so you can bookmark a list, the calendar or a single task.
