# Driving mode

When Tick notices you are driving, the screen switches to a bigger, simpler view, so you can see and tick off what matters with a glance.

## When it switches on
- Tick is open and you have been going faster than about 25 km/h for half a minute.
- It only works when Tick may already use your location (for example for [places](places.md)), or after you turn **Driving mode** on in [Settings](settings.md#display-this-device). Tick never asks for your location out of the blue.
- Standing still or walking for about 3 minutes ends the drive, and the normal view comes back. A traffic light or a traffic jam does not.
- As a passenger, or on a train, it switches on too. Use **Not now**.

## What you see
- A bar at the top: **Driving mode detected**, with two buttons:
  - **Not now** turns it off for this drive. On the next drive it comes back.
  - **Never** turns the feature off on all your devices. Turn it back on under **Settings → Display → Driving mode**.
- **Nearby:** open tasks with a place within 3 km, closest first.
- **Today:** open tasks due today or overdue, earliest first.
- How many other open tasks there are, and a big **Add task** button.
- Every task has a big round button to tick it off. Tap the title to open the task.

## Adding and editing
Adding a task and the task screen keep all their options (time, reminder, repeat, list, place, notes, subtasks, attachments), just bigger: larger text, taller buttons and fields. The screen gets longer, so you scroll. If a task is already open when the drive starts, it grows along and keeps what you typed.

## Your location
Tick uses your location only on this device, only while Tick is open, and only to work out your speed and what is nearby. It is not sent to the server and not stored.
