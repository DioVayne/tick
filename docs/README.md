# Tick user guide

How to use Tick, one page per topic. Installing Tick is in [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md); how it is built is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Tasks and lists](tasks-and-lists.md) | Adding tasks, subtasks, lists, pinning, dragging and swiping, search, notes, attachments, the trash |
| [Tickmanager](tickmanager.md) | The round along old tasks: what counts as old, the orange button, the four choices, skipping lists, the settings |
| [Reminders](reminders.md) | Reminders by time, repeating, the overdue nudge, the calendar, notifications on each device |
| [Linking your calendar](calendar.md) | Linking Google, Outlook or iCloud with a secret iCal address, free moments in the reminder menu and the Tickmanager, busy blocks in the calendar, Add to calendar |
| [Places](places.md) | Reminders on arrival or on leaving, more than one place per task, favorite places, the radius, notifying again, on time on the road |
| [Driving mode](driving-mode.md) | The bigger, simpler view while you drive, Not now and Never, adding and editing on the road |
| [Creating your own account](signup.md) | On an open Tick: making an account, confirming your email address, the 30 days, sharing by email |
| [Reporting and blocking](reporting.md) | On an open Tick: reporting a shared task, list, file or invitation, blocking someone, what the administrator can do |
| [Sharing](sharing.md) | Accounts for your household and a forgotten password, sharing a list, sharing a task or a copy, sharing on an open Tick, seeing how far others are |
| [Android app](android.md) | Download, linking, the home-screen widget, quick add, on time on the road, updates |
| [iPhone and iPad](iphone.md) | Putting Tick on the home screen, notifications, what an iPhone cannot do |
| [Settings](settings.md) | Display, language and time, devices, keys, backup and import, managing accounts |
| [Moving to another Tick](moving.md) | Taking your whole account to another Tick (to or from app.ticktodo.nl), what comes along, shared tasks |
| [Terms of service](terms.md) | On an open Tick: the terms you agree to when you create an account, in English and Dutch |
| [Privacy](privacy.md) | What Tick keeps about you, your location, the services it uses, and how to see, download or delete your data |
| [Integrations](integrations.md) | Claude (MCP), the inbox API for automations, Home Assistant, the fallback webhook |

The app itself has a short **ⓘ Help** page in the sidebar: one sentence per button, grouped into Basics, Organising, Time and Getting it back, the keyboard shortcuts (on a computer), and a link to this guide.
