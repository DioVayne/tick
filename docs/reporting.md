# Reporting and blocking

On an open Tick, where anyone can make an account (such as the one behind the Android app in Google Play), you can report something someone shared with you, and block a person. On a Tick for your own household these buttons don't exist.

## Reporting
You can report:

- an **invitation** to a task or list, next to **Accept** and **Decline**;
- a **task someone shared with you**, or a task someone else put in a shared list: **Report** at the bottom of the task;
- a **shared list** you are a member of: **Report list** in the list's menu;
- a **file** someone else added to a shared task: the ⚑ button on it.

Choose a reason (spam, inappropriate, illegal or something else) and, if you like, add a note. Then:

1. The reported task, list or invitation disappears from your Tick straight away: you stop sharing it. If you own the task and someone else added the file, that person is taken off your task or list instead.
2. **Also block** the person is ticked by default (see below).
3. The administrator of this Tick gets an email and looks at it. The email holds the reason, your note and the title, never the file itself.

You don't hear back about the outcome, and the person you reported is not told.

You can't report something you shared yourself, or a copy you accepted: that is your own task now, so delete it if you don't want it.

## Blocking
Block someone from the report window (**Also block**, or **Only block** to block without reporting). Blocking:

- stops everything you share with that person, both ways: their tasks leave your Tick and yours leave theirs. If one of you owns a shared list the other is in, the member leaves it. A list someone else owns stays as it is.
- stops their invitations from reaching you. They are not told: to them, an invitation looks sent, the same as to an address without an account.

Your blocked people are under **⚙ Settings → Account → Blocked people**. **Unblock** lifts the block; what was shared before does not come back, but you can share again.

## What the administrator can do
The administrator opens the report from the link in the email, or from **⚙ Settings → Accounts → Reports**: a page only they can see, signed in. There they see the reported content as it is now, with its files, and who reported it and who it is about. They can:

- mark it as **Nothing wrong**;
- **Remove content**: the task or file is deleted for good. A file also disappears from copies other people accepted;
- **Suspend** the account: it is signed out, can no longer sign in or use its keys, and everything it shared with others stops. Its own tasks are kept. The administrator can lift a suspension.
