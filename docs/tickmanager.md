# Tickmanager

The Tickmanager brings back tasks you have not looked at in a long time, one by one, so they do not disappear at the bottom of a list. For each task you decide what happens to it. It is on by default and works offline.

## What counts as old
A task comes back when you have not looked at it for a while:
- **A new task** is old after **14 days**.
- **A task you already looked at in a tidy-up round** is old again after **30 days**.

Both are in days (1 to 365) and you can change them on the Tickmanager page. A task that is exactly that many days old counts as old. "Looked at" is only about the Tickmanager: editing a task does not count, and a task you left as it was is not shown again until the longer term has passed.

These do not take part:
- tasks that are **pinned**,
- **repeating** tasks,
- tasks with a **reminder in the future** (an overdue reminder does count),
- **subtasks** (they go along with their main task),
- completed and deleted tasks,
- tasks in a list you skipped (see below).

The oldest task comes first.

## Where you find it
A number shows how many old tasks there are; at 0 there is no number.
- **Phone or narrow window:** an orange lightning button at the bottom left, opposite the **+**. It hides when a task, the card or the keyboard is in the way, like the **+**. The same item is also in the drawer (☰).
- **Wide screen:** an orange **Tickmanager** item in the sidebar under Inbox, always there, also while a task is open. When no task is selected, the empty panel on the right shows a card with a short explanation, the number of old tasks and the button **Start tidying up**. With nothing old it says that you are all caught up.

Tap the button or the item to open the Tickmanager page.

## The page
- **N tasks have not been looked at in a while** and the button **Start tidying up**. With nothing old: *All caught up*.
- **A new task is old after (days)** and **A task you looked at is old again after (days)**: the two terms above. Settings are saved when you leave the field.
- **Lists:** every list you can see, your own and shared ones, with a box **Include** and how many old tasks it has. Untick a list you do not want in your round, for example one called *Someday*. This is per person: it only changes your round. A list you just made can be skipped once it is saved (the box is grey until then).

## The round
**Start tidying up** shows one task at a time as a card: its title, its list, how long it has not been looked at ("Not looked at for 3 weeks") and the start of its notes. At the top is a counter ("3 of 8") and **Stop**. The tasks of the round are fixed when you start; stopping takes you back to the page and the rest stays for next time.

Each task has four choices:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Schedule** | Shows suggestions as buttons (*Today*, only when the default reminder time has not passed yet, *Tomorrow* and *Next week*, all at your default reminder time) and **Pick a date…** for the full picker. Choosing sets the reminder. *Cancel* (or clearing the date) leaves the card as it is. |
| **Move to top** | Puts the task at the top of its list, under the pinned tasks. |
| **Leave it** | Changes nothing except that the task counts as looked at. |
| **Delete** | Moves the task to the trash, with the usual *Undo*. |

Except for Delete (which the task leaves for the trash) and a cancelled Schedule, every choice marks the task as looked at. At the end you get a summary ("2 scheduled, 1 moved to top, 1 deleted, 4 left as is") and **Back to your list**; the number on the button updates.

## Shared lists
Whether a task was looked at belongs to the task, not to you: when someone in a shared list marks a task as looked at, it counts for everyone in that list. Which lists you skip is your own.

## Turning it off
**Settings → Display → Show Tickmanager** (on by default, for you on all your devices). Off removes the orange button, the sidebar item and the card; the address of the page then opens your normal list. The terms and skipped lists are kept for when you turn it back on. There is also a line about it on the **ⓘ Help** page.

## Offline
The number and the round come from the tasks already on your device, and your choices are sent when you are back online. Changing the terms, the skipped lists and the Show Tickmanager switch need a connection.
